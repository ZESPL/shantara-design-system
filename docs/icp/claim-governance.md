## 24. Claim governance

This ICP system is about **understanding demand**. It does not establish medical efficacy.

Do **not** encode marketing claims from external agency proposals as approved messaging. The following must **not** automatically become copy, ads, or titles:

- Disease reversal guarantees
- “Without medicine” as a promise
- Treatment of heart blockage
- Fixed success rates
- Claims that conventional medicine only manages symptoms
- Guaranteed biological-age reduction
- Cure claims

Those require separate clinical, evidence, and legal review. Where programme documents already use strong words (notably “reversal”, “complete healing”, “detoxification”), do not strengthen or propagate them through this ICP document.

If a claim cannot be sourced from approved Shantara clinical material, omit it.

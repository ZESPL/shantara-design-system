One large Light figure (48→84px, tabular) with a small caption.

```jsx
<Numeral value="25,000+" label="Guests since 2000" />
```

**Rule.** Two or three in a row, never four — a lone number on a second row is the failure this component exists to prevent. Use `NumeralsSplit` for the section. Figures come from `content/site.json`; never invent one.

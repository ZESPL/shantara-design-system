The deck's heading block: eyebrow → full-sentence Light headline → one quiet supporting line.

```jsx
<Statement eyebrow="How a programme works" sub="Progress is reviewed through the stay.">
  Each programme begins with a consultation and is adjusted as your stay goes on.
</Statement>
```

**Type.** Every size is Diodrum Light and fluid (clamp between a 360px phone and a 1440px desktop). The supporting line is body size — about a third of the headline — never a second headline. Always left-aligned.

**Measure.** Headlines wrap at 30ch; pass `measure` for a short page title.

**Responsive.** No breakpoints needed; `actions` stack full width under 520px.

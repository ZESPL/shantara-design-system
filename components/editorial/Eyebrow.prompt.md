The small tracked label above a heading. Colour comes from the ground: Himalaya on Merino/Stone, Gold on flat dark grounds, Merino on photography.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Medical leadership</Eyebrow>
```

**Rule.** An eyebrow must add information. Never repeat the heading beneath it ("ACCOMMODATION" over "Accommodation"). If it adds nothing, leave it out.

**RTL.** Tracking and uppercase are dropped in Arabic (tokens/rtl.css).
